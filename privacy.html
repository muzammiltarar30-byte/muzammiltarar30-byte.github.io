Trust Me Bro: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Trust Me Bro ("the game", "we") is a mobile game. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels finished, death counts, coins, hats/skins, button layout, sound settings and the nickname you type for friend rooms) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

If you allow notifications, the game may show an occasional reminder from the old man when you have not played for a while. These reminders are created on your device; no data is sent anywhere for them. You can turn them off in your Android notification settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information and ad interaction data to show, measure and improve ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Friend rooms (Google Firebase)

If you use "Play with friends", the game signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication (a random id, no name, e-mail or phone number) and uses Firebase Realtime Database to share, with the other players in your room only: the nickname you chose, your position in the level, whether you died or finished, and your score. There is no free text chat; players can only send preset hint lines and game "troll cards". Room data is removed when players leave the room, and rooms expire after 6 hours. To keep cheaters and fake copies of the game out of rooms, Firebase App Check asks Google Play Integrity whether the request comes from the genuine app. Firebase is operated by Google; see Firebase privacy information.

3b. Online duels and the speedrun leaderboard

Online duels and quick match use the same anonymous Firebase sign-in and share the same game data as friend rooms (nickname, position, finished or not), plus the preset chat lines you pick. If you finish a full speedrun and choose to upload it, the leaderboard stores your nickname, your run time and the date, visible to every player. A newer, faster run replaces your old one. To have your entry removed, e-mail us your nickname and run time.

4. Sharing

When you tap Share, the game creates an image ("I died N times") and hands it to the app you pick. When you share a death replay, the game makes a short video of your run on your device and hands it to the app you pick. We do not see what you share.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through friend rooms, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security and retention

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. Room data is kept only while the room exists (at most 6 hours). Ad data is kept by Google according to its own policies.

7. Your choices

  • Change ad consent: Settings → Privacy options (shown when it applies to you).
  • US residents (for example California): opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your data for ads in the same place, Settings → Privacy options ("Don't sell or share my data"). You then still see ads, just not personalised ones.
  • Delete local progress: Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the game.
  • Do not use friend rooms if you do not want to share a nickname and in-game position.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
